# Arturo Solo Strategy

## Target problem

Founders and operators already know a multi-source process is eating their time—for example assembling an investor-facing report from Notion, Excel, and form submissions that land in email—but they can't locate the real barriers to fixing it. So hiring a consultant, buying SaaS, or asking ChatGPT usually becomes a waste of time and money.

## Our approach

I run the whole engagement AI-first—AI helps me produce judgment *and* the brief—so the path from pain to decision stays clear and fast. I do not pitch AI as the answer to everything, and I do not produce a report without a decision path grounded in the assessment flow (reconstruct → compare simplify / buy / automate / build → decide before building).

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Decision-makers (or people with recommendation-level authority) in small businesses under ~20 people—typically a founder/president/CEO or an operations second-in-command, sometimes with a team manager implementing. They're hiring Arturo Solo to turn a complicated, time-consuming process into a repeatable workflow they can run themselves.

Small-business leaders are forced to wear many hats with disparate responsibilities, so their time is scarce. Efficient, self-operated workflows give them clear ROI: less time lost to multi-source grind, more capacity for the work only they can do.

## Key metrics

- **Assessment → justified custom build rate** - Share of Workflow Assessments that lead to a separately scoped custom build. Target: 20%. Today: ~1 of 3 active WA-adjacent clients returned for further custom-build work that is actively used. Measured: nowhere systematic yet (manual count). This is “build when earned,” not “upsell every assessment.”
- **Repeat engagement rate** - Share of clients with 2+ paid engagements (new problem, extension, training, or build). Today: ~10%. Measured: nowhere systematic yet.
- **Referral-sourced opportunities** - Count of live opportunities that came from a client referral. Today: 1 (custom-build discussion). Measured: nowhere systematic yet.
- **Actively utilized extension work** - Clients who return to extend a solution already in use. Today: 1 clear case. Measured: nowhere systematic yet.

## Tracks

### Clear decision path every engagement

Every engagement produces a grounded decision path—not a vague report or a pre-sold build.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Makes barrier-finding tractable and keeps “decide before building” real.

### AI-assisted delivery method

Strengthen the AI-first operating system: notes → judgment → brief → comparison/pros–cons.

_Why it serves the approach:_ That's how the whole engagement stays AI-first without pitching AI as the client's default answer.

### Client-owned handoff

Ship systems that can be handed off—reusable workflows clients operate themselves, not one-off task utilities.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Matches the job they hire for and supports repeat work, training, and referrals.
